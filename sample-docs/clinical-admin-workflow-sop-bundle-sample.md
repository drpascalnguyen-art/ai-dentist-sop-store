# Clinical Admin Workflow SOP Bundle - Sample

This sample demonstrates the style and scope of the administrative workflow templates included in the Clinical Admin Workflow SOP Bundle.

## Meeting Notes To Action Register SOP

Purpose:
Turn dental team meeting notes into clear tasks with owners, due dates, and next actions.

## Inputs

- Meeting date.
- Attendees.
- Discussion notes.
- Decisions made.
- Open loops.
- Follow-up owners.

## Output Format

| Action | Owner | Due Date | Next Action | Status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Document ledger posting procedure | Office lead | Friday | Confirm final steps and training gaps | Open |
| Review schedule bottlenecks | Doctor + office lead | Tuesday | Identify top two appointment types running long | Open |

## Quality Standard

Every action must have one directly responsible owner. If an item has multiple contributors, list one owner and name the supporting roles in the notes.

## Review Rhythm

- Review open tasks weekly.
- Close tasks only when the output is documented or assigned to a new workflow.
- Move unclear items into a parking lot instead of letting them disappear.
